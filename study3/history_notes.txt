History-length comparison: predeclared robustness and methodological review

Implementation: study3/history.py
Public API: analyze_history(per_user_df, candidate_frame, outputdir,
                           n_boot=1000, seed=20261114,
                           dataset_label="confirmation_v3")
Per-user fields: user_id, recall, repeat_targets; average repeated model seeds
within each user before calling. Candidate fields: user_id, history_orders,
optional product_id. Pass ALL historical candidates for exactly those users.

Six specifications (fixed before new study confirmation):
* Original: candidate bins 0,20,50,100,200,1000000;
            target bins 0,3,7,10,20,1000000.
* Finer: candidate bins 0,10,20,35,50,75,100,150,200,1000000;
         target bins 0,2,4,6,10,15,25,1000000.
* Coarser: candidate bins 0,30,75,150,1000000;
           target bins 0,5,10,20,1000000.
Each binning uses minimum 10 and 20 users per history group per cell.
History groups: 3-5, 6-15 and 16+ observed prior orders.
No specification is selected based on a favorable result.

Outputs separate three comparisons:
1. Raw means over all users with nonempty repeat targets.
2. Raw means over the exact common-support users used in standardization.
3. Means of per-cell Recall weighted by the pooled common-support cell sizes,
   with the SAME cell weights used for every history group.

The raw overall to raw common-support shift reflects sample restriction.
The raw common-support to standardized shift reflects changed cell weights.
These are exact arithmetic decompositions, not causal mediation effects.

Bootstrap: independent sampling with replacement WITHIN every history-by-size
cell, using 1,000 replicates and fixed observed support, cell sample sizes and
pooled weights. Report percentile marginal 95% intervals on long-minus-short.
The intervals condition on the fitted models and do not cover re-estimating
support/weights, retraining or model selection. The six sensitivity intervals
are not six independent confirmatory tests.

Review of the prior v2 result (exploratory; inspected 2026-11-14):
The original arithmetic is reproducible and correct. There is no evidence here
of an implementation bug. However, the common-support restriction retains
1,243/2,542 nonempty long-history users versus 1,992/2,032 short-history users.
Therefore, its adjusted chart describes a materially different population.

The six-specification posthoc rerun of v2 gives standardized long-minus-short
differences from +10.32 to +14.82 percentage points, with every marginal lower
interval bound above zero. All six reverse the raw overall -19.17 pp contrast.
But the finer/min20 common-support raw contrast is already +0.38 pp (interval
contains zero); this specification does NOT reverse direction solely through
reweighting. Do not imply that all of the overall reversal is due to weights.
Across specifications, retained nonempty users range from 4,197 to 5,882 out
of 7,486. Full exploratory output: outputs/study3_history_v2_exploratory/.

Interpretation limits:
* The next basket's repeat-target count is future/outcome information. It is
  used only in retrospective evaluation; it cannot be a prediction-time input.
* Conditioning on target size can induce selection effects. Coarse bins leave
  residual within-cell differences. This is not causal adjustment.
* Evidence of stable standardized direction supports caution about aggregate
  user-group comparisons. It does not prove that acquiring more history will
  improve recommendations by that amount.
* V2 reruns remain exploratory. Independent new-user confirmation with a new
  training sample strengthens replication but is still one platform/dataset.
